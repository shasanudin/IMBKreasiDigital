4. True DMG
Catatan True DMG: True DMG mengabaikan DEF, RES, dan Damage Reduction musuh, tetapi tetap terpengaruh oleh shield. True DMG dapat ditingkatkan oleh Melt dan Vaporize, tetapi tidak oleh Spread dan Aggravate.

📐 Referensi Rumus (Fandom Wiki):
• General DMG = [(Base DMG × Base Multiplier) + Additive Base Bonus] × DMG Bonus × Elevation × DEF × RES × CRIT
• Amplifying = Base DMG × [Reaction Coefficient × (1 + EM Bonus + Reaction Bonus)]
• Transformative = [Coefficient × Level Multiplier × (1 + EM Bonus + Reaction Bonus) + Additive Bonus] × RES × CRIT
• Catalyze = Coefficient × Level Multiplier × (1 + EM Bonus + Reaction Bonus)
• EM Bonus (Amplifying) = 2.78 × EM / (EM + 1400)
• EM Bonus (Transformative) = 16 × EM / (EM + 2000)
• EM Bonus (Catalyze) = 5 × EM / (EM + 1200)
• EM Bonus (Lunar/Stellar) = 6 × EM / (EM + 2000)
• DEF Multiplier = (LvChar+100) / [(LvChar+100) + (LvEnemy+100)(1-DEF Red)(1-Ignore DEF)]
• RES Multiplier: ≥75% → 1/(1+4×RES) | 0-75% → 1-RES | <0% → 1-RES/2
